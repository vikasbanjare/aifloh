body: |-
  1. One subject you can name in two words at 168 px wide.
  2. Five words or fewer of text.
  3. Faces at 28-40% of the height.
  4. Light on the faces matches the light in the scene.
  5. Nothing in the bottom-right timestamp corner.
  6. One accent colour, on one word.
  7. No AI tells: warped hands, garbled micro-text, plastic skin.
