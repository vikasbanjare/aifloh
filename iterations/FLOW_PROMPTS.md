## 4. Settings and checks

- **Model:** the highest-quality Veo model in Flow, at **1080p** (4K if offered). Use **16:9**; a 9:16 phone set is a bonus.
- **Audio:** not needed. The hero plays silently.
- **Generate 2–4 takes of each clip** and keep the best.
- **Reject a take if:**
  - the face or clothes change;
  - the room layout changes (the bed must stay left and the desk right);
  - there are extra people;
  - text or a watermark appears;
  - there's warped hands or typing.
- **Clock digits and screen text:** AI video garbles numbers, so keep them blurred. The site shows the real time as an overlay.
- **If Flow refuses a realistic face from your photo:** use the "No photo?" description instead. The rest stays the same.
- **Send me** all 8 clips at the highest export quality.

## 5. What I'll do with them

1. Edit the 8 clips into one cut, about 60 seconds, with matched colour.
2. Encode it so scrolling scrubs smoothly in both directions, with separate light desktop and phone versions.
3. Scroll plays the film in the existing sticky hero. The clock, captions and "Skip intro" stay, and the first frame becomes the instant poster.
4. Reduced-motion and slow devices get a still frame. Everything is self-hosted, with no YouTube or third-party player.
5. The current hero stays live until the video version is in and verified.
